Support

For help with ArtikelFit, email feedback.yourapp@gmail.com.

Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue.

Common Help

  • No account is required to use the app.
  • Progress is stored on the device.
  • If audio is muted, check the device volume and silent mode.
  • If the app behaves unexpectedly, close and reopen it, then try again.
  • Use “Restore purchases” on the Pro screen to restore a previous purchase made with the same Apple ID.
