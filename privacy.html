Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

ArtikelFit: Der Die Das is a German learning app. This policy covers local learning, optional Pro purchases and the invitation feature where enabled. Older free releases may not activate purchase or invitation services.

Learning data on your device

ArtikelFit stores learning progress, completed words, practice results, language preferences and daily goals locally on your device. The developer does not receive your lesson answers or learning history. Lessons do not require an account. Reset Progress in Profile removes learning progress from the device; deleting local app data removes remaining local settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Paid versions use Apple In-App Purchase and RevenueCat to offer Pro access, validate purchases, restore access and prevent fraud. RevenueCat processes purchase history, product and transaction identifiers, subscription status, an app user identifier and technical purchase information. Its dashboard also provides purchase analytics. The purchase SDK may contact RevenueCat when the app starts even if you do not buy. Apple processes payment; the developer does not receive your payment card or bank account details. RevenueCat initially uses an anonymous app identifier. Optional Apple sign-in for invitations associates purchase access with a pseudonymous account ID.

Optional Apple sign-in and invitations

Where the invitation feature is enabled, you can choose Sign in with Apple to keep an invitation code and reward across devices. The app requests neither your name nor email address from Apple. Our service verifies Apple authentication tokens and derives a pseudonymous account ID from Apple's identifier using a keyed hash. It records invitation-code use, invitation relationships, reward balances, offer reservations and redemptions. A matching purchase notification from RevenueCat confirms use of a reward. These records are needed to apply the discount once and prevent repeated claims. The feature does not upload your address book. Sharing a code uses the device share sheet and sends only the message you choose to share.

Security and storage

Referral sessions are stored in secure device storage and expire after 30 days. Authentication challenges expire after five minutes. Server session tokens are stored as hashes; Apple refresh tokens and redeemable offer codes are encrypted. The service processes network addresses and request information for rate limiting and security. The current test service is hosted on Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects and is separate from production Apple purchases. Feature availability and processing depend on the version you use; an unavailable invitation feature does not create a referral account.

Account deletion and retention

You can delete the optional invitation account from its panel in Profile. This revokes Apple authorization, removes server sessions and stored Apple refresh tokens, and retires the invitation code. It does not erase learning progress stored on your device or cancel an Apple subscription. Manage subscriptions in your Apple account settings. Within 30 days of account deletion, we remove invitation relationships and the retained encrypted offer-code value. We keep only the pseudonymous account identifier, whether a friend code was used, the earned reward count, reward assignment/redemption and purchase-transaction deduplication records needed to prevent repeated claims until the invitation program closes. These minimal records are deleted when the program closes. Account deletion does not restore an already used reward. Contact us to request access, correction or review of deletion of retained records. Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction records under their own policies and legal obligations.

Audio, advertising and tracking

Pronunciation in the production app uses bundled recordings and device speech features. Development-only speech generation services are not called by the production app. ArtikelFit does not show advertising, request the advertising identifier, sell personal data or track people across other companies' apps and websites for advertising.

Providers and international processing

Apple provides payment, subscription and optional sign-in services. RevenueCat provides purchase validation, entitlements and purchase analytics. Cloudflare hosts the current invitation test service. These providers may process information outside your country under their own terms and applicable safeguards. Opening a support page, privacy page or App Store link also connects to the destination service. Our public information pages are hosted on GitHub Pages.

Your choices and contact

Learning is available without Apple sign-in. Purchases and invitations are optional. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, restrict or object to processing, obtain a copy of data, and complain to a data protection authority. We use purchase and account information to provide requested features and security records to prevent fraud. For privacy requests and support, contact Andrii Kostenko, the ArtikelFit developer, at feedback.yourapp@gmail.com. Do not include passwords, payment card details or Apple authentication codes in a support message.
