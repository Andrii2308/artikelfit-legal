Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

ArtikelFit: Der Die Das is designed as a local learning app. It does not require an account and does not collect information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores learning progress, completed words, practice records, and daily goal settings locally on your device. This information is used only to show progress and personalize practice inside the app.

Purchases

When you view, buy, or restore ArtikelFit Pro, Apple processes the payment and RevenueCat processes purchase history, receipt information, basic device information, and an anonymous app user identifier. This information is used for purchase validation, fraud prevention, restoring purchases, entitlement delivery, and aggregate purchase analytics. ArtikelFit does not receive your full payment card details.

Audio and Speech

The production app uses local app audio and device speech features to pronounce German words. Development-only audio tooling is not included as a cloud service in the App Store production build.

Tracking and Advertising

The app does not track users across apps or websites and does not show advertising.

Third Parties

The app uses Apple App Store services for payment and RevenueCat for purchase validation and entitlement management. It does not use advertising SDKs.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact feedback.yourapp@gmail.com.
