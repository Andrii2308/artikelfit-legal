Privacy Policy Last updated: October 24, 2026 ArtikelFit: Der Die Das is designed as a local learning app. It does not require an account and does not collect information for advertising or cross-app tracking. Data Stored on Your Device The app stores learning progress, completed words, practice records, and daily goal settings locally on your device. This information is used only to show progress and personalize practice inside the app. Purchases The current free-launch release does not offer purchases or subscriptions. The RevenueCat purchase SDK is not initialized, and no purchase history, receipt or anonymous RevenueCat user identifier is sent by this release. All topics and practice modes are temporarily free. A future paid release will update this policy and explain any purchase-related processing before it is enabled. Audio and Speech The production app uses local app audio and device speech features to pronounce German words. Development-only audio tooling is not included as a cloud service in the App Store production build. Tracking and Advertising The app does not track users across apps or websites and does not show advertising. Third Parties The current release does not activate payment, advertising or analytics SDKs. When you choose Support or Rate App, the app opens the linked external website or App Store, whose own privacy terms apply. Contact For privacy questions or support, contact feedback.yourapp@gmail.com
